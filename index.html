FOR TEACHERS

Use this lesson with your class

This site is a teaching resource. Google Forms alone receives student submissions. To keep each class’s responses in its own teacher account, create a Form using the questions-only teacher sheet, then create a class link here. If the owner has granted you access to the private Form template, you can make your own copy of it instead.

  1. Open the exact 25-question sheet and place those items in a Google Form you own.
  2. In your Form, open Responses and link it to a new Sheet in your account if you want a spreadsheet. Check its access settings.
  3. Publish your Form for your pupils, then paste its full docs.google.com viewform link here.
  4. Share the generated class link. Students submit to your Form, and only your Form stores their answers.

Keep your response Sheet private. The shared question sheet contains no answer key or pupil data.
